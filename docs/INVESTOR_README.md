# Crypto Hound — Investor Snapshot

Crypto Hound is a regulator‑grade, quantum‑secure compliance and governance platform that automates cryptographic sealing, audit trails, and regulator‑ready reporting for institutional crypto businesses.

## Why it matters

- **Institutional trust**: auditable, tamper‑evident compliance bundles (weekly) reduce counterparty and regulatory risk.
- **Market**: servicing exchanges, custodians, and regulated funds who must provide provable evidence of controls and treasury movements.

## Product highlights

- **Sealed compliance packs**: PDFs, cryptographic signatures, and optionally blockchain anchors.
- **Treasury forms automation**: auto‑filled government forms and submission tracking.
- **Regulator dashboard & investor subscriptions**: read‑only evidence access, subscription revenue via Stripe.

## Business model

- **B2B SaaS**: monthly subscription for institutions + optional auditor/multi‑seat licenses.
- **Add‑ons**: on‑demand attestation, custom anchoring, white‑glove compliance integrations.
